# Gate 1 v2.3 Semantic Invalidation

Gate 1 v2.3 retains a structurally valid I/E/R chain, but its evidence is not semantically
sufficient for a product decision. Its historical decision is therefore not usable.

The invalidation is limited to experiment semantics: handwritten H1 changes were not paired with
equivalent ABI changes; H2 configuration and cleanup artifacts did not prove distinct executions;
H3 reused aggregate evidence; and hypothesis failure transport was conflated with apparatus
invalidity. Gate 1 v2.4 supersedes those experiment semantics without deleting or rewriting the
v2.3 commits.
